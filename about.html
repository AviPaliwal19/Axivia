The Legacy

Three decades before Axivia, there was Paliwal Properties.

Founded by Mahesh Paliwal, Paliwal Properties served Raipur's real estate community for over thirty years — built on handshakes, honest dealings, and the kind of reputation that doesn't need a marketing budget. Axivia is the next chapter of that legacy, founded by his son Avi Paliwal, who carries forward the same principle: your word is your foundation.

The Journey

From the lens to the land.

Avi's path to real estate was anything but conventional. He spent years as a photographer — living quietly, seeing the world through frames and light. That artistic life taught him patience, precision, and an eye for what most people overlook.

Photography led to event management, where he learned to deliver the impossible on an unforgiving clock. Orchestrating large-scale events sharpened something in him — the ability to hold complexity together under pressure, with no room for excuses.

But real estate was in his blood. He founded Arise Constructions Pvt Ltd, taking on government infrastructure — roads, bridges, community centres — and proved he could build at scale. Every discipline he had touched was preparing him for what came next.

The Problem

He saw the rot because he lived through it.

In 2023, Avi set out to build his own home. What should have been a straightforward project became a years-long education in everything broken about Raipur's real estate market — false commitments, sub-standard materials, vendors who vanished after taking the money. The very industry he grew up in was being hollowed out by people who treated homebuyers as targets, not clients.

He wasn't the only one. Everywhere he looked, families were being misled — promised dream homes and handed compromises. The market didn't have a trust problem. It had a trust crisis.

The Pledge

Axivia exists because someone had to fix this.

In 2026, Avi pledged to do what no one around him seemed willing to — make real estate in Raipur reliable, transparent, and honest. Not with slogans, but with systems. Not with promises, but with proof.

Axivia was built to be the brokerage that stands between you and the chaos — verifying what others won't, questioning what others accept, and refusing to let a single client walk into a commitment they'll regret. Every property is vetted. Every vendor is accountable. Every word is meant.

If you wouldn't sell it to your own family, don't bring it to ours.
— The Axivia Standard
